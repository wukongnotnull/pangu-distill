## 工程口语

> I think it's also important to reason from first principles rather than by analogy. … The normal way that we conduct our lives is we reason by analogy. … it's kind of mentally easier to reason by analogy rather than from first principles. … first principles is kind of a physics way of looking at the world … you kind of boil things down to the most fundamental truths and … say okay what are we sure is true or as sure as possible is true and then reason up from there.

— Elon Musk，Kevin Rose 访谈，2012-09。[视频](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L-s_3b5fRd8)

## 批评

> The price of the materials, for instance, assumes a certain supply and demand balance. … Since the price is based on an assumption … it can hardly be called a first principle.

— [Jefago](https://www.jefago.com/product-management/the-problem-of-first-principles-thinking/)

> Sometimes the assumption you are challenging might be more fundamental than it seems at first. Sometimes you might replace one flawed assumption with another.

— 同上。

> human societies have one characteristic that makes modeling them with universal laws impossible: reflexivity. Social structures are constructed out of meaning. … they … cease to exist once people stop believing in them.

— Lasse Gerrits, *Social Physics in the Age of Generative AI*。
